# Within-parent prediction analysis implementation

`transfer_alignment.prediction_analysis.analyze_prediction` accepts unique parent/candidate records containing alignment, cosine, update norm and an independently obtained outcome change. It computes tied average ranks and within-parent Spearman correlations. Raw scores/outcomes are never pooled across parent offsets.

The descriptive `alignment_all_informative_parents` field preserves alignment correlations wherever alignment and outcomes vary. Comparator permutation diagnostics use the common subset where all three predictors and outcomes vary, with equal weight per parent. Excluded parents and constant fields remain explicit. A `not_estimable` status concerns the common comparator analysis; inspect the alignment-only descriptive field separately. Flat outcomes are not evidence that alignment has zero predictive value.

The two-sided Monte Carlo permutation test shuffles outcomes within each parent, uses fixed seed 20260915, defaults to 5,000 draws and uses the plus-one correction. Comparator p-values are unadjusted secondary diagnostics, not pairwise tests of predictor superiority. Its exchangeability assumption must be justified for the frozen random candidate design; correlated/adaptive candidates or dependent parents can invalidate it. The analysis does not propagate uncertainty of estimated rewards/gradients or establish scene independence. Independent probe/outcome identities and provenance must be checked outside this function against the frozen experiment manifest.

Five local tests passed: tied ranks; opposing within-parent effects despite large pooled offsets; flat outcomes; shared comparator denominator while preserving other informative alignment parents; deterministic permutation output; and duplicate/nonfinite rejection.
